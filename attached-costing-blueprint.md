# Attached Costing Blueprint

The attached Arabic blueprint requires five cost elements: direct materials, material overhead, resources/labor and machines, manufacturing overhead, and outside processing. It requires costing methods: standard costing, perpetual average, periodic average, and actual FIFO. It also requires cost types/scenarios for actual, standard, budget, and simulation costs; financial integration across inventory, WIP, purchasing, and general ledger; and analytical reports for inventory valuation, material price/quantity variances, resource rate/efficiency variances, accumulated production-order costs, and product profitability.

The product coding rule requested by the user is: codes beginning with 1 are raw materials, 2 are semi-finished products, and 3 are finished products. The system must provide BOMs, manufacturing operations, work orders, cost-center distribution, detailed cost/profit reports, and end-to-end database/API/UI verification.
